# Kickstarter Kickstarter

A reusable, evidence-gated campaign starter for hardware, software, sideload apps, SaaS, and hybrid products.

## Start here

1. Copy `CAMPAIGN-BRIEF.template.md` and answer every decision marked `[DECIDE]`.
2. Enter reward assumptions in the blue cells of `Kickstarter-Kickstarter-workbook.xlsx`.
3. Put every material promise in the Claim Ledger. A claim without evidence does not reach the campaign page.
4. Draft the page with `CAMPAIGN-PAGE.template.md`.
5. Use `ASSET-SHOT-LIST.template.md` to plan real proof, not decorative footage.
6. Pass every gate in `LAUNCH-GATES.md` before scheduling launch.

`campaign.schema.json` is a portable structure for future automation. `campaign.example.voiceish.json` shows one compliant example; it is a discussion draft, not approved campaign copy.

## The operating rule

Separate four things that campaigns often blur:

- what exists now;
- what is prototyped and evidenced;
- what funding will build;
- what depends on a carrier, platform, supplier, regulator, or partner.
